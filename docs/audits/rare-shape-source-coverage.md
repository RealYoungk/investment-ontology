# Remaining rare source shapes

The original 241-failure snapshot contains 54 receipts outside the five main period/header/core/issuer/API-period groups. All 54 exact receipt shells and own annual cover/CFS/OFS bodies were fetched from the official DART viewer; the full bytes, source coordinates and hashes are retained in `outputs/filing-collection-priority/rare-shape-source-corpus` in the user's workspace. Original error fields in retrieval metadata are historical inventory fields, not retrieval failures.

Current normal OFS parser replay yields 11 positive bases after the blank-CFO-caption repair. Three newly repaired NovMetaPharma bases additionally passed normal admin database ingest and idempotent replay. Other parser positives are still candidates, not a claim of complete validation or production recovery. One positive is a partial six-account financial statement and remains subject to the separate storage guard review.

Remaining original shapes include duplicate company/trust statements, missing printed units, ambiguous current monetary groups, source dates that conflict with independent equity statements, malformed amounts and unusually wide cells. In particular, Korea Real Estate Investment & Trust prints both company and trust balance sheets: selecting the first matching table alone is insufficient. A distinct issuer/basis/whole-statement proof is required. No real source contradiction, unavailable total or unstated monetary unit is replaced by zero, a prior amount or an inferred value.

The machine ledger explicitly distinguishes retrieval, parser replay, normal-storage verification and production recovery. Whole individual text review remains incomplete and is not represented as finished.
