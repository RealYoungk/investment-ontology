# Business original metadata anchor

Business-only explicit attachment corrections may register an existing nonempty original business document's source filing metadata. No original business text is copied or relabeled under the correction receipt. Normal 28-section collection is unchanged.

The bounded candidate is one existing same-company/year/report-kind original whose filing ledger is absent. Official correction shell must identify the company and its latest same-kind family body must be that older receipt. Official original shell and annual/half cover must bind the company, reporting kind and actual end date. Stable official corporate identifiers on both target and original shells bind identity. Observed cover company name and its comparison are audited; mismatches warn without modifying company master or guessing a new legal identity. Wrong/missing/ambiguous corporate identifier or actual period yields explicit businessSourceMetadata failure.

Public requests are keyless with one-second spacing, <=3 GETs per candidate; unsupported quarter/q3 paths are excluded. Cancellation is checked before fetch, after proof and in transaction. Normal transaction rechecks existing filing, target and original document, then registers metadata-only Source audit and source DartFiling. Financial source statuses start pending; latest-period/final-revision selection keeps this older same-period original outside fresh target scope. Target business ledger can then use the unchanged original document through existing source-period coverage rules.

No manual DB write, production deployment, document-number forgery or source-value replacement is performed by this patch.
